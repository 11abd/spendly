Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and password. We also collect the financial data you enter, such as expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to help us maintain and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Spendly service
  • Generate your expense summaries, categories, and reports
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Communicate with you about your account or service updates
  • Improve and develop new features for the service

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, and you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third Party Services

Spendly may use trusted third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, or email delivery. These providers only have access to the information necessary to perform their functions and are obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes.

We do not share your financial data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings. You may also contact us directly to request a copy of your data or ask questions about how it is handled.

7. Cookies

Spendly may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may affect some functionality.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.app
